Console

>Keyboard at the speed of thought.

Console runs Hercule the way you already run your tools: from the keyboard. The command bar is the front door. Screens are numbered panes that tile, split and pop out. Every action shows its key and every decision is a verb: s start, a accept, d dismiss, . repeat. One status line along the bottom carries the whole swarm. Color is syntax: each theme is a sixteen-color terminal palette, and every slot has one job, everywhere.

Why this one

When agents are cheap, the scarce thing is the gap between seeing and answering. Console closes it to a keystroke. Every row has a cursor, every answer a key, every pane a number, and your last answer is one . away. It scales by tiling, not scrolling: Intake left, the Proposal under the cursor right, a run's log below, all live. Its themes are whole palettes, not recolors, so yellow means "needs you" in a diff, a meter, a notification and the menu bar.

what it trades awayA gentle first minute. A newcomer meets keycaps before meaning. Console pays that back with labels that never disappear, a ? sheet of every key and mark, and a command bar that takes plain words. For one calm decision at a time, pick Docket. For forty things at once, pick this.

The system

A terminal's discipline with an app's manners. Everything below is live and follows the theme you pick at the top.

Color doctrine: color is syntax

16 slots · 1 job each

A theme is a terminal theme: a ground, an ink, and sixteen slots. Every hue on screen is one of those slots, and every slot has one job, the way a syntax theme gives every token type one color. Learn a color once and it means the same thing in a diff, a meter, a notification and the menu bar.

  • Ink is for words, hue is for meaning. A word is never colored for decoration.
  • A row carries one state hue and one project hue at most. Shape tells them apart: a state is a mark, a project is a square.
  • Yellow means "this needs you". Nothing else is ever yellow. The mark says how: ? waits on your answer, ! is degraded, || is paused until you act.
  • sMagenta is the cursor: focus, keys, the selected row, the primary answer, the mode chip. One hot thing per pane.
  • Sources are shapes, not colors. Sentry, Gmail and GitHub are monochrome marks in ink.

At 10x

Ten times the work: 140 live sessions on nine machines, sixty Proposals, eleven decisions waiting. The screen gets denser, not longer, and the keys stay the same.

Groups, not pages. Triage folds look-alike Proposals by cause; one answer can close a group.

The office zooms out. Rooms shrink to their cells; the waiting balloons stay the same size.

Notifications batch. Eleven waiting decisions are one notification with a count, not eleven pings.

What this design asks the spec to change

Console changes presentation, not semantics. Where it needs more, or where the fixture and the spec disagree, it says so here.

1spec 14 · App shell, top baradditiveA ⌘K command bar in the top bar.

Go to, answer or start anything in plain words. Every item carries its key, so the bar teaches the keyboard.

2spec 14 · App shell, sidebar footchangesThe pulse becomes the bottom status line; fleet meters sit in the sidebar foot.

The status line shows mode, context, the swarm, warnings, the keys that work and the clock, on every screen.

3spec 14 · App shell, theme row (#186)changesSix themes plus "follow the system" as a day and night pair.

Instead of Light, Dark and System: the default pair is Paper Tape by day and Phosphor by night.

4spec 14 · IconographyadditiveEvery nav item carries a glyph and its chord.

Intake is g i, Runs is g r, and so on; the sidebar shows them.

5spec 14 · The Intake view, the check-in viewchangesThe row under the cursor opens in a split second pane.

The Proposal page stays for deep links, and it is where a shared URL lands.

6docs/design-language.mdchangesThe primary answer's key is filled magenta, and the fonts change.

This replaces quiet ghost buttons. Onest and IBM Plex Mono become Mona Sans and JetBrains Mono.

7spec 06 §8 · spec 14 composerquestionA default access mode per provider instance?

The fixture shows one per instance; the spec keeps the default on the thread config and on Agents. Settings · Providers draws the fixture; the spec should decide.

8spec 12 §8.3conflictThe fixture's recurring Reminder is ruled out by the spec.

"Mondays 08:30, weekly revenue summary" is a recurring Reminder, which spec 12 excludes. Console draws it as the fixture asks; one of the two should change.

9spec 10 §5conflictThe fixture's "paused" Run for "Label new issues".

The spec pauses the trigger, not a Run. Console draws the hold on the trigger, with its held events; the fixture should say so too.

10spec 14 · V1 screen inventory, spec 10 §7.3additiveA native mobile client.

A tab bar with Check-in, push notifications with bound actions through a push sink, and a Live Activity for a working thread.

One fixture quirk every design shares: the lead Proposal is still in Intake although a thread started from it at 09:02. Console keeps it there, as the fixture asks.
