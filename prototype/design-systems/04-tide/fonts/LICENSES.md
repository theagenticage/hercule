# Fonts used by Tide

Both families are self-hosted woff2 files (latin subset), downloaded once from fontsource's CDN.
Nothing is fetched from a third party at runtime.

| Family | Files | Licence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sora (variable, weights 100-800) | `sora-latin-wght-normal.woff2` | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | Google Fonts, via fontsource `sora` |
| DM Mono (static 300, 400, 500) | `dm-mono-latin-{300,400,500}-normal.woff2` | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | Google Fonts, via fontsource `dm-mono` |

Sora: Copyright 2019 The Sora Project Authors (https://github.com/sora-xor/sora-font).
DM Mono: Copyright 2020 The DM Mono Project Authors (https://www.github.com/googlefonts/dm-mono).

The full licence text is at https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/ and applies
to both families: the fonts may be used, embedded and redistributed freely, and may not be sold on
their own.
