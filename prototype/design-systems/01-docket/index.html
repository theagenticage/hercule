Docket

A design system for Hercule · 01 of 10

The unit of the interface is a decision.

Hercule's agents do the work; you make the calls. Docket turns every call into one card on one docket: a Proposal, an Offer, a Request from a session, a tripped breaker, anything triage could not decide. One card is in focus, its answers on single keys. The rest wait in a fan behind it, counted and colored by kind, and a rail of ticks records each verdict. Transcripts, settings and the fleet are reference, one step away.

02

Why this one

The other nine organize what Hercule shows: lines, rooms, streams, a crew, a console. Docket organizes what you do. Your morning is a pile of calls, so the home screen is that pile, one card at a time, with a finish line. The count reaches zero and the design tells you how long it took.

It is the one layout that gets shorter as the agents get better. More agents means more cards, and cards fold: by question, by cause, by Topic. The rest of Hercule stays as it is today: the thread surface, Made from, and the calm where nothing shouts unless it matters.

What it trades away

Browsing. Docket is a to-do machine, not a map. Wandering through what your agents did happens on plain reference pages, not on home. If you want to watch the swarm hum, pick another direction. If you want to be done by 09:43, pick this one.

The system

Swiss grid, hairlines, strong numerals. Color is used boldly and only where it means something.

Color doctrine

Three families that never mix. One color never means two things, and state is never color alone.

The rule. A verdict color never marks a kind; a kind color never marks a state. Every verdict also has a mark and a word.

Themes

Two light, three dark. Click one to switch the whole book, every frame included.

No dark ground sits in hue 220-275. Body text is at least 4.5:1 in every theme; verdict and kind colors are re-tuned per ground so green, amber and red keep their weight. The keys light and dark point at Paper and Graphite.

Geist and Geist Mono, self-hosted (OFL). Words stop at 20px; numerals go to 88. Hierarchy comes from numbers, not headings.

09

What this design asks the spec to change

No record changes. Every item below is about where things show and how they are answered.

  1. Home is the docket

    After the first triage run, the default route is Intake. On a fresh install, with nothing triaged, Sessions stays home as today.

    Spec 14 · V1 screen inventory"Home is Sessions"
  2. One docket for every open decision

    Proposals, Offers, unsure items, breakers, permission Requests and agent questions share one queue. Check-in keeps "In motion"; its Focus card becomes the docket card.

    Spec 14 · Post-v1"both views stay separate in v1"; check-in principle 4
  3. Answers are verbs on keys, in a row

    Each answer shows its label, key and describe line in one row. The producer's description moves into the card's gist and the opened card.

    Spec 14 · The check-in view"Answers as a ledger"; spec 10 §7.4
  4. The suggested answer is the primary one

    When triage offers Start X, it marks that answer primary, so it lands on Y and is solid green. Accept stays primary when there is no Start.

    Spec 10 §2.4"Accept (primary)"
  5. Later is a docket move, not an answer

    Later sends a card to the back of your docket and changes no record. There is still no park. It looks like a verb, which the spec forbids for navigation.

    Spec 10 §2.4"There is no park"Spec 14 · The check-in view"Navigation is never an answer"
  6. Decision speed replaces the headline sentence

    "14 decided in 1m 52s" is derived from each Resolution's at and actor. The counts move to the docket count and What came in. Urgency shows by position and priority bars, so there is no pulsing dot.

    Spec 14 · Intake principles 1 and 3spec 10 §7.1, the record
  7. What came in as numeral columns

    One column per source with its count and what it became, instead of one row per Connection. The per-connection events view is unchanged.

    Spec 14 · Intake anatomy
  8. One page per decision for outside links

    A link from Slack or email opens one card as a page. Inside the app, detail still opens over the page, never as a split.

    Spec 14 · Intake principle 7"Detail lives in a drawer"
  9. Group answers and folded Proposals at scale

    One answer for several identical questions runs notification.act once per record. Proposals with a shared cause fold into one card by their provenance.

    Spec 10 §7.4Bound actionsSpec 10 §4Proposal and Topic
  10. Native shells

    A desktop app with a global hotkey, menu bar extra and actionable notifications; a phone app with a Live Activity.

    Spec 14 · Packages and desktop-shell readinessand Post-v1, "Desktop app"
