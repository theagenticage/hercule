# Fonts in Concierge

All three families are self-hosted woff2 files, latin subset, variable weight axis, downloaded
once from fontsource (`cdn.jsdelivr.net/fontsource/fonts/<id>:vf@latest/latin-wght-normal.woff2`).
Every one is licensed under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1** (https://openfontlicense.org).

| File | Family | Role in Concierge | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `newsreader-latin-wght.woff2` | Newsreader (Production Type) | An assistant's voice. Upright only; the italic is never loaded | OFL-1.1 |
| `inter-latin-wght.woff2` | Inter (Rasmus Andersson) | Every piece of UI: labels, objects, numerals | OFL-1.1 |
| `jetbrains-mono-latin-wght.woff2` | JetBrains Mono (JetBrains) | Code, branches, commands, timestamps | OFL-1.1 |
