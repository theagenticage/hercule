Hercule · Aurora
Why 1000x System Screens Office At 10x Adopting Spec

Aurora

Calm glass, living light.

Hercule as frosted glass over a soft field of light. The field is the system's mood: a slow green drift while agents work, warming to amber as decisions wait, a coral bloom where something burns. Light comes from the side of the screen where the thing lives, so you feel the state before you read a word. Violet is Hercule's own light. Everything else is quiet glass and ink, in five themes - two light, three dark, none of them navy.

02 · Why this one

Light is read before words are

Attention is the scarce thing, and reading is the most expensive way to spend it. Aurora moves the first read from words to light: before you parse a row, the field has told you whether to look, and where. Light works in peripheral vision - on a second screen, across the room, on a lock screen.

It is also the most native of the ten. Vibrancy, depth and slow motion are how macOS and iOS already speak, so the desktop app and the phone feel at home, not ported.

  • Trades density. Big radii and generous space fit fewer rows than a console.
  • Trades contrast headroom. Panes stay opaque enough for 4.5:1; a solid mode replaces glass.
  • Trades decoration. Light only works while it means something, so nothing glows for style.
  • Not Halo, not Tide: no ring glyph, no time axis. Light is the one idea.

The first five changes

In order; each ships on its own and leaves the app working.
  1. Swap the tokensReplace the theme blocks in packages/ui/src/styles.css with tokens.css: five themes on html[data-theme], light and dark as aliases, fonts self-hosted. Settings gains Appearance to pick one.
  2. Add the fieldA Field component in packages/ui draws lights it is given. Which lights to draw is a client-core function over the attention summary, so every screen agrees.
  3. One glass paneA Pane primitive with thin, regular and strong, and the solid swap for reduced transparency. The sidebar, composer, popovers and drawers adopt it; nested cards go.
  4. Marks with lightRedraw packages/ui/src/marks/marks.tsx on the 16px grid. Add Orb, Disc and lit priority bars, so every state is a mark, a word and a light.
  5. Decisive answersThe ledger's primary row is lit and keyed; the composer shrinks and clears on scroll; the Request dock is lit amber. Then the tab title and favicon carry the Check-in count.
09 · What this design asks the spec to change

Semantics stay; these are the declared changes

  • Color at pane scaleThe pinned language keeps color to words and dots and forbids washes and filled buttons. Aurora lights panes, washes counts, lights priority bars in urgency color and fills the primary answer in violet.spec 14 · Design language
  • Settings gains two screensAppearance (five themes, follow system, transparency, motion) and Providers (instances and their defaults). Connections and Machines are also listed there, opening /connections and /fleet.spec 14 · V1 screen inventory
  • A Proposal has a page/intake/<proposal id>, so a link from Slack or email lands on one decision.spec 14 · URL space
  • The web shell folds at 1280The two-faced sidebar becomes a rail of marks with counts; the Threads face moves into the title bar as tabs.spec 14 · App shell
  • All sessions gets List | OfficeThe office is a second view of the same page, with the same lanes and answers.spec 14 · App shell
  • The pulse is openThe sidebar foot shows the pulse as a bar and words, not a closed line. The Marks legend toggle is not drawn in these mockups; it would sit beside the settings button.spec 14 · Sidebar foot
  • Batch answers at 10x"Accept 25 low" and grouped calls answer many Proposals at once. That needs a batch operation, or a client that fans out and reports each result.spec 10 §4 · spec 11
  • The phone opens on IntakeOn mobile the tab bar leads with Intake and Check-in; Threads is third. On the desktop and web, Sessions stays home.spec 14 · Home is Sessions
  • Native surfaces need native pushGlance assumes the desktop app. Answering from the lock screen and the Live Activity need a native iOS app with push, beyond Web Push.spec 14 · Post-v1
