Postmark

Everything that arrives gets stamped.

Hercule is a sorting office for work. Thousands of events arrive and triage decides what each one became. Postmark draws that literally. Every Connection is a postage stamp, every verdict a rubber stamp in one of four inks, every project a sorting tag. A Proposal is an envelope that carries the stamps of everything that made it, and answering it franks it. Paper and ink, crisp rather than quaint, in five themes on desktop, web and phone.

02

Why this one

Postmark borrows the product's own vocabulary. Triage already stamps every event: proposal, attached, offer, FYI, unsure, held, no action. Drawn as stamps, the metaphor costs nothing to learn.

Every item answers three questions in the same three shapes: where from (postage stamp), what it became (rubber stamp), which project (sorting tag). Colour never floats free, so it never needs a legend.

It scales by piling: 212 events are one row of stamps; 31,000 are the same row with bigger numbers.

What it trades away
  • Neutrality. A tilted red stamp is louder than a 6px dot, and may feel playful for an ops tool.
  • Craft. Ink texture, perforation and postage art are custom parts, not a kit.
  • Not speed. Every stamp is also a word, and every answer one key.
03

Why it is 1000x better than today

Today's design has good bones, and Postmark keeps them: the centred thread, "Worked for 31s" dividers, the changed-files card, the permission dock on the composer, and the rule that nothing shouts unless it matters. What changes is how fast you see and how fast you decide.

1

You see the verdict before you read a word

Today every row is grey text with a 6px dot, so finding the one that burns means reading all nine. Here the red stamp finds you, the postage stamps say where it came from, and the sorting tag says which project. Attention goes to the decision, not to parsing.

2

A dark theme that is not navy

Hue 265 at chroma 0.029 reads as navy and blurs ground, card and text into one murk. Carbon leans toward violet at chroma 0.012, with three clear steps of paper and text at 4.5:1 or better. Four more themes, two of them dark and neither blue.

3

Answers that land

Today a decision is three ghost words. Here it is three keyed rows, each with the describe line of what the click does, and one filled primary. Answering franks the letter with a thunk and the browser tab changes with it, so you know it took and move on.

4

Decide where you are

Today Hercule is one web tab. Postmark adds a phone that answers from the lock screen, and a menu bar and quick panel on the desktop that answer without switching apps. The same stamps everywhere, so nothing is relearned.

5

A pile that deepens, not a list that grows

Today every session is a row, so 140 sessions are 140 rows. The office puts them in rooms per runner with a flag over every desk that waits on you, and the flags are what you look for. Intake does the same with stamps: volume raises a number, not the page.

06

The office

The sessions overview, drawn as the sorting office the whole system describes. It is the same data as the list, and one toggle away from it.

Mail comes in on the wall

The Connections' postage stamps hang on the back wall with today's counts, and the postmark of the last triage sits above the desk that sorts them.

Flags are your call

Each runner is a room of desks, one per slot. A lid lit violet is an agent at work; an ochre flag with a ? is one waiting on you, labelled with what it asks.

Pigeonholes are Intake

Triage files into pigeonholes by tier: Now, Needs a call, Today, When you can, FYI. The assistants stand at the counter, asleep or awake.

07

At 10x

140 live sessions and 31,000 events a day. Postmark never answers volume with a longer list.

Denominations, not rows

A Connection is one stamp whatever its volume. Sentry goes from 131 to 19,400; the album keeps seven stamps.

Stacks, with one answer

Proposals that share a verdict and an action stack into one envelope with a count: 12 dependency bumps, merge all. You open a stack only to make an exception.

Only waiting stays tall

Rooms fold into blocks and desks into bricks, but the flags keep their height, so 11 flags up still find you among 140.

09

What this design asks the spec to change

  • 14 · Design language Colour at stamp scale, and one filled answer. Spec 14 keeps colour at word and dot scale and makes decision affordances text-only ghost buttons. Postmark gives colour to three shapes and fills the one primary answer of a decision.
  • 14 · App shell, theme row Five named themes plus System, instead of Light, Dark and System. Light and Dark stay as the names of Manila and Carbon.
  • 14 · V1 screen inventory, URL space New paths: /proposals/<id> (one Proposal as a page, where a non-interactive sink's deep link lands), /sessions/<id> and /sessions/new for shareable threads, and /settings/providers.
  • 14 · App shell The tab carries state. The document title counts open decisions and the favicon carries a red or ochre dot. It needs no new data: the counts are the ones the sidebar already shows.
  • 10 · 7.3 Sinks · 14 · Post-v1 A phone. The mobile pages assume a native app: an interactive push sink that renders bound actions as notification buttons, and a Live Activity for a working thread. Spec 14 rules out Web Push in v1 and lists it after v1, so this is post-v1 work.
  • 14 · V1 screen inventory On the phone, home is Intake, not Sessions: a phone is where you decide more than where you start work. The web and desktop keep Sessions as home.
  • 10 · 7.4 Bound actions A swipe is an informed click. Swiping a postcard answers it, so the answer row with its describe line must show under the card before the swipe commits.
