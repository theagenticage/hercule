# Fonts

Both fonts are self-hosted from this folder. The pages make no font requests to any other host.

| File | Family | Axes | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `overpass-var.woff2` | Overpass | wght 100-900, latin subset | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | Google Fonts via Fontsource (`overpass`, v19) |
| `overpass-mono-var.woff2` | Overpass Mono | wght 300-700, latin subset | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | Google Fonts via Fontsource (`overpass-mono`, v21) |

Overpass is Copyright 2016 Red Hat, Inc., designed by Delve Withrington with Dave Bailey and Thomas
Jockin. It is an open typeface inspired by Highway Gothic, the lettering of North American road
signs, which is why Metro uses it: it was drawn to be read at a glance, at a distance, in motion.
