Metro Concourse

A glass concourse over the network.

Concourse keeps what made Metro the strongest brand: the signage, the ink line, and events you can watch move. It folds the network map into one slim departures board and uses that board wherever flow matters. Everything else gets quieter. The shell is Aurora's: a floating sidebar, header pills and composer in subtle glass over a plain ground. Icons are conventional, state is six marks, signs are Overpass and sentences are Inter. Nothing glows.

2

Why this one

Over the original, and over its sibling, Metro Wayfinding.

Pick Concourse if you liked Aurora's shell and Metro's brand and want both at full strength. Over the original, it keeps the signage and the moving events but puts them in one place, the departures board, so every other screen reads as plain text on a quiet ground.

Over Wayfinding it goes further: the sidebar floats, the header is pills with no bar, glass starts at 0.6, and sentences are set in Inter. Wayfinding is the safer pick if you want less glass and one typeface.

  • Glass costs contrast. At level 1 the panels hold their 70% fill, but they are busier to look at. The frames under Glass show the limit.
  • Two faces to load. Overpass and Inter, plus Overpass Mono for code.
  • The map is gone. The board shows counts per line, not the path of each event. The path lives on the Proposal, as "Made from".

Overpass, for signs

Titles, navigation, labels, buttons and every numeral. Overpass comes from Highway Gothic: letters made to be read at speed, which is how you read a sign or a count.

Since deploy #1289, card payments that need 3-D Secure fail.

Inter, for sentences

Messages, descriptions and the gist of a Proposal. In a paragraph Overpass's wide, round letters tire the eye; Inter is narrower and more even, so a sentence reads in one pass.

Overpass Mono, for code only

Code blocks, commands, diffs and branch names. Never a label, a time or a count: those stay in Overpass, with tabular figures so columns line up.

Three depths, the same ones the screens use. A row is flat, with a hairline under it. A card adds a hairline around it and a contact shadow. Only what floats gets the light rim and the long shadow, and in a dark theme a fill one step lighter. No card sits inside a card.

Glassone level, 0 to 1; 0.6 by default

What may be glass

  • Layers that float over content: the sidebar panel, the header pills and tabs, the composer, the board where it floats, menus, the quick panel, sheets, notifications, the office's panels and the phone's tab bar.
  • Never rows, cards, messages or tables. A list never turns into a pane of frosted tiles.
  • What shows through is content: the transcript, a list, the office. Never a gradient put there to feed the glass.
  • Text on glass sits on a fill of at least 70% at level 1, and keeps 4.5:1 over a code block. At level 0 the blur is off and every panel is solid.
  • Reduce transparency, in Settings or in macOS, forces level 0.

Every value comes from one number

--glass-level: 0.6;
--glass:      color-mix(in oklab, var(--glass-base)
                calc(100% - 30% * var(--glass-level)), transparent);
--glass-blur: calc(14px - 8px * var(--glass-level));
--glass-sat:  calc(1 + 0.25 * var(--glass-level));
--rim:        color-mix(in oklab, var(--glass-rim)
                calc(55% + 45% * var(--glass-level)), transparent);

No refraction, no streaks, no rainbow edges. A light hairline inside the edge, brighter along the top, and a soft shadow.

Motion

Two things move, and both mean work is happening. The working mark turns once every 1.6 seconds. On the board, a small car runs along the Connection lines into Triage every 3.2 seconds. Both stop with Reduce motion. Nothing pulses and nothing glows.

What this design asks the spec to change

Five asks. Everything else is presentation over operations the spec already has.

A per-Proposal addressA link from Slack or email opens one Proposal as a page. The URL space has /intake but no address for one Proposal; add /intake/<proposal id>.spec 14 · URL space
Counts per Connection and outcomeThe board needs, since the last Triage the user saw, the event count per Connection and per outcome, when Triage ran and how long it took, and when it runs next. Today's Intake brief has the outcome totals only.spec 10 · Intake · spec 11 · a read
A hue per Connection typeConnection types are defined by plugins, so a type needs a hue: a palette slot in the plugin manifest, or a client-core fallback.spec 05 · manifest · spec 08 · Connection
The tab as a signalPin the tab title as "(n) screen · Hercule", n being the decisions waiting, and a favicon that gains a dot while n is above zero.spec 14 · App shell
Mobile opens on IntakeHome is Sessions on desktop and web, as pinned. On a phone the first tab is Intake, because that is where decisions get made on the go.spec 14 · Home is Sessions
Nothing else changes semanticsTheme and glass level are per-device Appearance preferences with no operation. The mobile app, Live Activities and native notifications are post-v1 clients of the same public API; every button is a Notification's bound action.spec 01 · out of scope · ADR 0012
