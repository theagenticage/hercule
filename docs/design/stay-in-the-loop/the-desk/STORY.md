# The Desk - Tuesday 29 September

PROTOTYPE. A walk through Rogier's day with the desk.

**09:02.** The Sentry alert about EU cards is burning. Rogier hands it to a thread and gets back to
his own work. In the next half hour he hands off six more from Intake: Pieter's bug, Marta's review,
the failing checks on #1300, Marta Visser's invoice, Jonas's refund question, the backup proposal.
Each leaves Intake at the click. Nothing pings him after.

**09:31.** One thing does. The bell in the Office's header swings once: the 3-D Secure thread wants
to run `git push`. A card drops under the bell, but it never takes the keyboard, and Rogier is deep
in a refactor. He lets it wait; the bell keeps its marigold count.

**09:41.** A focus block ends. He opens Office › Your desk. Four folders lie in the in-tray, two with
a small marigold dot: landed, not seen. Along the top, three colleagues are still out: two
Investigate faces typing, and the 3-D Secure thread with its hand up. He clicks it, reads "Pushes
fix/3ds-eu-cards to rogier/webshop from studio-mac, this once", and presses Allow once.

The top folder is Fix bug's pull request, #1302. The paper shows the cause in one line, the
three-line diff, and a quiet note: you signed off #1287 yesterday at 19:12. This is where it came
from. Under it, Pieter's reply is drafted and ticked to go with the merge. The first ledger row
reads "Squash-merges #1302 into main in rogier/webshop as GitHub · rogier, then replies to Pieter in
#acceptance as Slack · rogier". He presses ⌘↩. A lacquer stamp lands, SIGNED OFF, Merged · replied
· 09:41 · R, and the folder slides into the out-tray. The next one is already open.

It is the failed one, with a torn corner: Fix bug gave up on #1300 because it may not edit
`.github/workflows/test.yml`. He presses B. A note slip clips to the folder: "You may edit
.github/workflows/test.yml". ⌘↩, and the folder goes back out with its colleague.

Marta's invoice reply comes next, and it is wrong. A tinted note pinned near the top says what the
agent was unsure about: it guessed "Brightline Ltd" and attached the old invoice. He presses B and
writes: "It's Brightline B.V. (KvK 81234567). First names, short. Reissue it as INV-2291-R in Stripe
and attach that." The folder leaves.

Marta's review at #1294: approve with two comments, a high-confidence seal. ⌘↩ posts it. The trust
ledger on the right ticks Review PR to 15 in a row.

**09:42.** The bell swings again: Draft reply, attempt 2, wants to void INV-2291 and create
INV-2291-R in Stripe · live. It touches money, so it asks. N,
⌘↩: Allow once. The folder he was reading stayed open.

**09:43.** Jonas's refund findings drop into the tray with a soft slide. "Yes, Stripe allows it", the
one-line fix, and the answer it would post on PAY-212. ⌘↩. A minute later the invoice reply is back
with a "2nd draft" tab. He presses D and sees the change word by word: "Dear Ms. Visser" struck,
"Hi Marta" in green, INV-2291-R.pdf attached. ⌘↩ sends it. Fix bug's second try lands too: one
commit on #1300, checks green. S.

On top of the tray sits a folder from Hercule: Let Review PR post without stopping at your desk.
It says plainly what that is: a last step added to the workflow, posting as GitHub · rogier, saved
by him. He presses S, not now. Maybe next week.

**09:47.** The backup findings land. Two ways out, measured on a copy, and the agent says so. ⌘↩
hands it to Fix bug with the first one. The blotter is bare: a big Deco 7 and Desk clear.

What he did not do: open a single run page, scroll a transcript, check Gmail Drafts, find out at
four o'clock that a run had failed at 09:26, or explain the invoice again in a new thread.

**17:30.** Before leaving he sits down once more: three folders, two minutes.

After a month: the bell rings twice a day, the tray is cleared three times, and Review PR posts by
itself, because he chose that on a Tuesday when it had earned it.
