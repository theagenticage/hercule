# The Range - notes for the window builder

PROTOTYPE. `expedition-office.js` + `expedition-office.css`, mounted as `OfficeModes.expedition`. Develop and shoot it alone in `office.html` (`?state=`, `?theme=`, `?select=`, `?chrome=off` hides the switcher, `?motion=off` stops ambient life).

**What it shows.** A floating diorama island. One low-poly massif per project, in its tint (webshop sandstone, payments-api lavender, ops sage). Every lead's goal is its own peak, and **every summit stands at the same height**, so altitude is progress and the range is a bar chart with scenery on it. Contour lines at 25, 50 and 75% are the chart's gridlines; snow starts at about 75%. Each route is a dotted trail that zigzags up to its summit: the climbed part is in the lead's hue, the rest is faint. There is a tent at every camp (done: in the hue, with a pennant; the camp it heads for: in the hue; future: pale canvas) and a swallowtail flag on the summit (brass when landed). The lead and its partners climb as a rope team on a real rope. Working climbers walk uphill, idle ones sit on a rock, and a climber waiting on you raises a hand beside a marigold flag. A stuck team sits under a grey cloud, and an idle landed lead sits by its summit flag. Base camp is a real kit room: Your desk, "Now serving 4" (3 threads + the run), Ada, Milo and Juno. Its terrace holds the answered questions with no project (tBun, aRust, aTables). The runs are a mule caravan on the valley road, each a visored driver with a pack mule; the publish step waits there with a marigold flag.

**Labels.** Level of detail follows camera distance. Far: the massif pills ("WEBSHOP 4 routes · 1 needs you · 1 landed") and only the marigold asks. Mid and near: every team's tag ("Fix 3-D Secure checkout for EU cards ●● / camp 4 of 9 · Push the branch / ⚑ Run git push?"). Once the camera rests, a declutter pass lifts overlapping tags (with a leader line), keeps every tag inside the canvas, and folds crowded far chips into marigold pips.

**Signature moments and URLs** (`office.html?...`; the same states work through `desktop.html?mode=office&state=...`):
- `state=everyday`: the establishing shot. The hero is `shots/office-hero.webp`; `theme=orient-express` is the evening (headlamps, lit tents, lit lodge), `shots/office-dark.webp`.
- `select=t3ds`: the 3-D Secure rope team at camp 4 of 9, the lead waving by its marigold flag, review and research roped behind (`shots/office-team.webp`, phone crop `shots/office-phone.webp`).
- `state=stuck`: frames Migrate ops dashboards under its cloud, "no progress in 34m" (`office-stuck.webp`).
- `state=grew`: frames the Result migration; its two added camps glow with a pulsing ring, "+2 camps" (`office-grew.webp`).
- `state=away`: a "09:01" ghost ring where each moved route stood, the stretch climbed since then drawn in bright, larger dots, and "+5 camps since 09:01" or "new since 09:01" (`office-away.webp`).
- `state=swarm`: 19 routes; massifs narrow their spurs to keep their width, and the pills say "+31 more" (`office-swarm.webp`). `state=open` frames t3ds.

**Interaction.** Click a climber or its tag: `onSelect(id)`, a ring, the tag outlined, a glide. A second click within 450 ms: `onOpen(id)`. Click a massif (its pill or its rock) and the camera flies to it; click the lodge or its pill and it flies to base camp. "Whole range" (bottom right) clears the selection. `select()` takes thread ids, run ids or run-step session ids (`runShip`/`rShip`), and `assistant:Ada`; an unknown id or `null` shows the whole range. In a wide window a selected bean sits right of centre, clear of your 380x150 card. The legend hides below 1000 px. The app's camera controls are kept.

**Costs** (measured). The terrain is one merged flat-shaded mesh, about 65k triangles. Tents, flags, trees, ropes and mules are 3 vertex-coloured batches, trail dots are one instanced mesh, and colour comes from the tokens, repainted on a theme change with no rebuild. The whole view draws in 221 draw calls and 244k triangles (swarm: 303 and 368k), mostly the beans and the lodge's kit props. A team close-up draws in 33 calls. Idle cost is the app's: 30 fps of bean breathing, plus the cloud's bob and the grown camps' pulse. With reduced motion, working climbers stand and the stage draws **0 frames** once settled. Nothing changes per token: a route moves only when a camp is reached. `pause()` and `resume()` are the stage's own. `setState` rebuilds the island (fast on a GPU, about 1 s in headless SwiftShader).

**Limits.** "Failing CI is weather" is not drawn, because no route in the world has failing CI. A run's position on the road does not encode its step; its tag says "step 2 of 4". The lodge's walls follow the kit's cutaway rule. Hues come from `Crew.lookFor(title)`, the same rule as `expedition-route.js`, so beans match your faces. Two partners can share their lead's hue (the t3ds team has two sky beans).
