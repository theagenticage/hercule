# The Aerodrome - notes for the window builder

PROTOTYPE. `window.OfficeModes["control-tower"].mount(container, api)`, as BUILDERS.md says. Load `control-tower-data.js` before it (call signs, urgency, slots, the conflict) and `crew.js` (faces' hues); it reads them so its words and counts match the strips.

**What it shows.** Where a plane is says when its thread needs Rogier. Holding (open Request): circling the tower, oldest lowest, marigold beacon on the fin, its ask in a numbered column beside the stack. Approach (needs him within 15 min): on the glide path north of the runway, placed by ETA, with brass gates at 5, 10 and 15 min and the ETA range as a tube in the plane's hue. Cruising: loops over its project's hangar. On stand (unread result): stands 1-8 by the terminal, a paper on the wing and an ink dot on the tag. Parked: on its hangar's apron. Done: inside the hangar, no pilot. Runs: mail planes circling race pylons, one per step (green band = done, marigold lamp = waiting on you); the waiting run step flies in the holding stack as PA·SHIP. Assistants sit on the terminal's roof. Rogier's desk and the Now serving sign (= holding count) are in the tower cab.

**Signature moments.** `landing`: WS·3DS leaves the stack, touches down on runway 18 and climbs back to its loop over the webshop hangar (about 20 s), with a dashed track; the planes above it step down a level. `conflict`: the three 10:02-10:06 arrivals fan out on the extended approach inside a soft marigold ring. `slots`: held asks turn grey and say "lands 10:00"; the urgent push stays marigold. `away`: a card counts take-offs, landings and new holds since 09:01, and dashed taxi tracks show the landings. `moving`: WS·CART 2 lands and taxis to a stand.

**Handle extras.** `clear(id)`: call it when Rogier answers a Request in any mode; the plane does the touch-and-go. `land(id)`: call it when a turn ends (your `landByItself`). `frameZone(name)`: field, holding, approach, conflict, stand, parked, hangar, mail, `hangar:<project>`. `setState` resets both. `select(id)` glides to the plane and follows it while it flies; null flies home. A click on a plane or its tag calls `onSelect`, a double-click `onOpen`. A click on a hangar or the tower flies there.

**HUD.** Top left: camera stops only (Aerodrome, Holding stack, Approach, Stands, Hangars, Air mail); the counts are yours. Below it a 272px state card in away, conflict, slots and landing. Bottom left stays clear for your card; the conflict view is framed for it.

**Best shots** (`office.html?state=...&motion=off&switch=off`, 2x, `--wait 14000`): `everyday` (hero), `theme=orient-express`, `zone=holding`, `conflict`, `swarm`, `slots`, `away`. `landing&at=5` or `at=9.5` freezes the touch-and-go mid-flight. Captures are in `shots/office-*.png`.

**Costs** (measured in headless Chromium). Everyday: 24 planes, 470 draw calls with the shadow and output passes, 313k triangles; swarm: 43 planes, 718 calls, 482k triangles. One stage, the kit's 4096 shadow map, drawn once. With motion: the planes fly continuously at the stage's 30 fps cap while visible; nothing draws while hidden or after `pause()`. Reduce Motion (`api.reducedMotion`): every plane parks in its phase, and 0 frames were drawn in 8 s idle. Tags: about 45 DOM labels; a per-frame declutter hides the less urgent of two overlapping tags (holding first, then soonest ETA), with no layout reads after a tag's first measure.

**Limits.** Labels for the holding levels sit at each level's height, not on each plane, so the number links ask to level. The app's doze animates even with ambient motion off, so under Reduce Motion an asleep assistant sits still instead. Planes do not avoid each other; loops are tuned so they rarely cross.
